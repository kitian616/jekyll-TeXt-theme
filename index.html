About the Lab

Founded in 2010 at the National University, our group focuses on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and High-Performance Computing. We are a diverse team of researchers, PhD students, and undergraduates committed to scientific excellence and open-source contribution. Our work is supported by national grants from CONICET and international collaborations.
